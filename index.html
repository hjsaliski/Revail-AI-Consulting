A fair question

"Isn't this just off-the-shelf automation?"

Third-party integration tools are great until the logic gets conditional. They move data between apps well. They don't read a photo, summarize a job, or decide what a client actually needs to see. That's where the AI layer comes in.

If a $20-a-month automation already solves your problem, we'll tell you that instead of building something custom. We only build what's genuinely missing.
